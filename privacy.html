Privacy Policy

Take Home Pay Calculator UK — Last updated 25 November 2026

Introduction

Take Home Pay Calculator UK is developed by Cosmic Production d.o.o., based in Split, Croatia. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data Collection

This app does not collect or store your salary inputs on our servers. Calculations are performed locally on your device.

iPhone and iPad

The Apple version uses the App Store and StoreKit for distribution and the optional one-time Premium purchase. We do not run our own analytics service in the app. We may review aggregated App Store Connect data provided by Apple. Apple’s privacy policy

The App Store privacy label for version 1.1.5 says “Data Not Collected”. This version removed the advertising SDK. Earlier Apple versions that included Google AdMob could use it to show ads in the free app. Google could process device identifiers, consent choices and ad interactions when serving ads in those versions. Google’s privacy policy

Android calculations and settings

The Android app calculates your pay on your device. It does not send the salary, pension, tax or student loan figures you enter to us. The app stores preferences such as its language on your device. Your Android backup settings may allow eligible app data to be backed up or copied when you change devices.

Android advertising by version

Android 1.0 (version code 1). This version includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. It contacts Google for your advertising consent options and may show a Google consent form. That request can include app, device and language details and previously stored consent choices. If the consent update fails, this version can still start the advertising SDK. Google’s advertising services can receive your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location, app and ad interactions such as views and taps, device identifiers, and diagnostics such as app performance. Google uses these data to deliver and measure advertising, analyse performance and prevent fraud. Some installations may also send SDK-related crash details, including a stack trace, to Google for diagnostics. The advertising SDK encrypts data in transit. You can manage your advertising ID in your Android privacy settings. Google’s advertising SDK disclosure · Google’s privacy policy

Android 1.0.1 (version code 2). This version removes Google AdMob, its consent SDK and the advertising-ID permission. The Android advertising practices described for version 1.0 apply to installations of that older version.

Google Play purchases on Android

Google Play processes the optional Premium purchase and handles your payment details. The app uses Google Play Billing to check ownership, acknowledge purchases and restore access. Google’s billing library may send technical information to Google about billing operations and errors. We do not receive your payment card details or send your salary inputs to Google Play Billing. Google’s privacy policy

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data. For questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at the address below. Apple and Google also explain how to manage data associated with their services in their privacy policies.
